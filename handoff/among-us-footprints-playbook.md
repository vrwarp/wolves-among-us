## 4. COUNSELOR ROLES

### Counselor 1 — Ghost Guide *(High School boys room)*
Receives dead players. Hands out origami **one sheet at a time**. Enforces fold quality before stamping. Keeps the room quiet — dead players do not shout information toward the hallway. Sends everyone back to the lobby when a meeting is called *(they attend but do not speak or vote)*.

### Counselor 2 — Roaming Referee *(hallways)*
Verifies portable tasks on the move: sudoku, mazes, location codes, bead names. Enforces walking. Breaks up groups larger than 2. **Is the counselor the imposter stabs to trigger Hush Lights Out.** During Hush, stands in the darkest hallway.

### Counselor 3 — Foreman *(physical task station)*
Runs cups, apples, and the airplane flight zone. Verifies and stamps instantly, then makes the student reset the station before leaving. Also runs the bonus pool (water bottle, sharp shooter, beads).

### Counselor 4 — Central Command *(lobby)*
Guards the button. Runs both stopwatches. Moderates every meeting. Maintains the **public death count** on the whiteboard. Runs the Hush Lights Out item list. Backup verifier for anything portable.

### Index card layout
Print each student's group list with a **blank box beside every task**. Add a **TOTAL** box at the bottom. One stamp = that task is done. Nothing else goes on the card.

> **Card inspection:** Imposters collect **real stamps** for tasks they actually complete. Their points simply don't count toward the win condition. This removes the "show me your card" tell entirely — an imposter's card looks normal.

## 7. TASK GROUPS *(replaces Slide 46)*

**Now 16 groups, not 4.** Full card contents are in the companion file `task-cards-16-groups.md`.

Every card carries **16 points** against a 10-point quota. The slack is deliberate — a jammed station must never lock a student out, and multiple 10-point routes per card keeps students spread across the building.

Each card follows the same shape: **1 hard (5) + 3 medium (3) + 2 easy (1)**.

Why 16 rather than 4: with 4 groups, five students hold identical cards, spot it immediately, and travel as a pack. With 16, only four groups are duplicated across 20 students — there is almost no one to herd with. This is the cheapest counter to the Buddy System in the whole design.

Deal cards face-down as students arrive. Give duplicated groups to students who are **not** friends.

### Station load

| Station | Share of task slots |
|---|---|
| Stage — Foreman | 29% |
| Center — Referee | 28% |
| Rooms 1 & 3 — self-serve codes | 28% |
| Room 2 + lobby | 15% |

No card carries more than two Foreman tasks.

### Bonus pool — open to everyone, unlimited
Available all game, but the real purpose is to give early finishers a reason to keep moving.

| Bonus task | Pts | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Spell your full name in beads on a pipe cleaner | 5 | Foreman |
| Water Bottle Flip (land upright) | 3 | Foreman |
| Sharp Shooter | 3 | Foreman |

**Bead task workflow:** take one pipe cleaner → thread letters from the pre-sorted cups → bend both ends over to lock → wear it as a bracelet → show any counselor. Hands stay free for other tasks.

## 8. CORE RULES

1. **No running.** One foot on the ground at all times. Applies to *everyone*, including someone who just found a body.
2. **Always carry your spoon.** Everyone has one. This is what makes imposters invisible.
3. **Touch = shoulder or upper back only.** No grabbing, no tackling, no chasing.
4. **Students do not close doors.** Counselors may close a door and reopen after 5 seconds. A closed door still counts as a hallway for movement.
5. **Groups of 2 maximum.** The Roaming Referee breaks up packs of 3+.
6. **Assisting = coaching only.** You may talk a friend through a task. You may **not** do it for them. The student earning the point does the folding, stacking, and threading.
7. **No phones.** Nothing in this game requires one.

---

## 9. IMPOSTER RULES

**The kill.** Tap a crewmate on the shoulder or upper back with your spoon. Say nothing. Keep walking.

**Reload.** After a kill you must **silently count to 20** before you may kill again. *(The original "walk through a door" rule was a 1–2 second cooldown and allowed near-instant double kills.)*

**Blending in.** Do tasks. Get real stamps. Nobody can tell your card from a crewmate's.

**Mission Critical — Hush Lights Out.** Up to 3 per game.
1. Imposter taps a **counselor** (the Roaming Referee) with their spoon.
2. The counselor waits a few seconds, then begins **dimming** lights. Other counselors join in.
3. Central Command reads a randomized item list aloud.
4. Crew has **1 minute** to bring every item to Central Command.
5. Fail = imposters win the round.

> Use this deliberately against the Buddy System. When the crew starts moving in pairs, Hush scatters them.

---

## 10. WHEN YOU DIE

1. **Lie down where you were tapped.** Silent. No pointing, no eye contact, no laughing.
2. **Maximum 60 seconds on the floor.** If nobody finds you, get up quietly and walk to the dead room yourself.
3. **The instant you hear "Emergen-C,"** stand up and walk directly to the **High School boys room**. Do not linger to hear the argument.
4. In the dead room: fold origami for points. **Green = 1, Blue = 3.** One sheet at a time from the Ghost Guide.
5. **You still need 10 points.** Dying slows you down; it does not excuse you.
6. You attend meetings but **do not speak and do not vote.**

## 11. EMERGEN-C & MEETINGS

### Finding a body
1. **Say nothing at the scene.**
2. **Walk** — not run — to the lobby.
3. The moment you cross into the lobby, yell **"EMERGEN-C!"**
4. Everyone who hears it echoes it and walks to the lobby. The Roaming Referee echoes it toward the far corners of the building.

*Why: yelling at the scene tells the whole building which hallway the body is in, which destroys every alibi in the game.*

### The button *(Judge, Jury, Executioner)*
- **3 per game, total, for the whole youth group.** Three physical tokens sit on Central Command's desk. To press the button you surrender a token. When the tokens are gone, the button is dead.
- Locked for the first 60 seconds — Central Command keeps a box over it.
- Body discoveries are unlimited and cost no token.

### Meeting protocol — 3 minutes, hard stop

**0:00–0:30 · The Report.** Total silence. The finder states three things only: where the body was, who they saw nearby, who they suspect. Interrupting costs you your vote.

**0:30–2:00 · Nominations.** No shouting names. You must say: *"I nominate ___ because ___."* Central Command counts down from five — if nobody says **"I second that,"** the accusation dies and the room moves on. Phase ends at 3 seconded nominations, even if time remains.

**2:00–2:30 · The Corners.** Nominees are sent to separate corners. Everyone else is silent. Each nominee gets **15 seconds** to answer their accuser.

**2:30–3:00 · Four Corners Vote.** Central Command: *"Ten seconds. Walk to a corner to eject that person. Stand dead center to skip."* Count down from 10. Anyone still drifting at zero loses their vote. Counselors count bodies. Largest corner wins. Ties = no ejection.

**Ejection.** The ejected player goes to the dead room and starts origami. Central Command **resets the 5-minute doom timer** and updates the whiteboard death count.

> Central Command must be ruthless about the clock. If students learn the timer stretches when the argument gets good, your whole night's pacing collapses.

## 12. WIN & LOSS CONDITIONS

**Crew wins** when every crewmate — living and dead — has 10 points.

**Imposters win** if any one of these happens:
- **6 cumulative deaths** *(recommended for 20 players; see dial below)*
- A Mission Critical fails
- The crew doesn't finish within **5 minutes of the last Mission Critical**
- Imposters equal or outnumber living crew

### The death-count dial

| Crew size | Threshold |
|---|---|
| 15 | 5 deaths |
| 17 | **6 deaths** |
| 20 | 7 deaths |

Realistic kill capacity in an 8-minute round is 4–8 deaths, so a threshold much above 6–7 is unreachable and imposters will visibly give up. Set it low for round 1; it is far easier to raise a threshold than to lower one after the imposters have stopped trying.

**Post round 1 adjustment:** imposters got 2 kills → drop to 4 · imposters hit 6 by minute five → raise to 8.

**Keep the count public.** Central Command writes each death on the lobby whiteboard. Visible dread is the whole point — the crew has to decide whether stopping to argue is worth the floor time.

## 13. THE BRIEFING *(8 minutes, and not one minute more)*

Do not explain the whole ruleset. Explain what they need to start moving. The rest they learn by playing.

1. **"Everyone has a spoon. Three of you are imposters."**
2. **"Get 10 points. Your card lists more than 10 — pick any combination."**
3. **"Find a counselor to stamp each task. Four counselors, four different stamps."**
4. **"You get killed by a tap on the shoulder. Lie down. Stay quiet. Then go to the High School boys room and fold origami — you still need your 10 points."**
5. **"Find a body: walk to the lobby, THEN yell Emergen-C. Never yell at the body."**
6. **"Walk. One foot on the ground. Always."**
7. **"If the lights dim, listen for Central Command."**

Brief imposters **privately** before the room fills. Tell them: *do real tasks, get real stamps, count to 20 between kills, and target anyone standing alone.*

---

## 14. SAFETY

- **Dim, never dark.** A one-minute scramble through a pitch-black building with a 6th-through-12th age spread is where someone gets hurt. Dim the lights, hand out 3–4 flashlights, keep the Roaming Referee stationed in the darkest hallway.
- **No running is a safety rule, not a flavor rule.** Enforce it hardest during Hush Lights Out and during Emergen-C transit, when adrenaline is highest.
- **Shoulder tap only.** Say this out loud in the briefing. Six-foot high schoolers and 11-year-olds are playing the same game.
- Apples go on a towel or tray. They will fall constantly and they roll.
- Know where your exits and stairs are before you dim anything.

## 15. COUNSELOR QUICK RULINGS

| Situation | Ruling |
|---|---|
| "Can I see your card?" | Allowed — imposters have real stamps, so it proves nothing |
| Student won't do any tasks | Referee walks them to a station. Tell imposters to target loiterers. |
| Pack of 4 moving together | Referee splits them. Max 2. |
| Someone finished all 10 points | Send them to the bonus pool. They keep moving. |
| Two students claim the same kill | Both are dead. Move on. |
| A student runs | Verbal warning, then a lost point. |
| Argument over a stamp | Counselor's stamp is final. No appeals. |
| Imposter tags a counselor who isn't the Referee | It still counts — start the Hush sequence |
| Dead player talking in the dead room | Ghost Guide pauses their origami for 30 seconds |
| Tie in the Four Corners vote | Nobody is ejected. Back to the floor. |
