# TASK CARDS — 16 GROUPS

*Among Us: Footprints Edition · print one card per student*

Every card totals **16 points**. Each student needs **10**. They choose the route.

Deal one card per student. With 20 students, four groups will be used twice — give the
duplicates to students who are not friends with each other.

---
