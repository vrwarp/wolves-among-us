# PRINT ME FIRST

**Among Us: Footprints Edition** — everything to print, in the order that matters.
Ordered by *what breaks the game if it's missing*, not alphabetically.
**CARDSTOCK** items are flagged. Everything else is plain paper.
The rules live in **`FACILITATOR-PLAYBOOK.pdf`** — print one per counselor who wants paper.

---

## 1. Task cards — the game does not exist without these

**`01-index-cards/index-cards-96-deck.pdf`** · 48 sheets · **CARDSTOCK**

Two cards per sheet. **One straight cut down the middle** — no small trimming.
Gives you 96 cards: three decks of 32, with `deck 1 / 2 / 3` marked on each cut line.

- Deal deck 1 for round 1, **deck 2 starting from group 11**, **deck 3 starting from group 21**, keeping students in the same order. Nobody gets the same group twice, so nobody carries door codes forward.
- Deal face-down as students arrive.
- `01-index-cards/index-cards-32-unique.pdf` (16 sheets) is the master, for reprints.

## 2. Door code sheets — every single card needs these

**`05-doors/door-code-sheets.pdf`** · 7 sheets · plain

One per door. **Tape all four corners** — these get grabbed.
Doors are labelled **U1 U2 U3 U4** upstairs and **D1 D2 D3** downstairs. The sheet *is* the door sign; there is nothing else to print for the doors.

## 3. Counselor answer sheet — nothing can be verified without it

**`07-counselor/answer-sheet.pdf`** · 4 pages × **4 copies = 16 sheets** · plain

All four counselors on the floor carry all four pages. Before the doors open, write on **every copy**:

- **TONIGHT'S TARGET** — the number you announce in the briefing
- The Bible line is pre-printed: **ESV LARGE PRINT pew** — large print and regular print have DIFFERENT page numbers, so the exact edition matters, not just "ESV"

## 4. Station signs

**`06-signs/station-signs.pdf`** · 9 pages, **print the SPECIAL DELIVERY page twice** = 10 sheets · plain

`CUP STACK · APPLE STACK · FLIGHT ZONE · SUDOKU · MAZE · BIBLE TABLE · GOSPEL & THEME · DEAD ROOM · SPECIAL DELIVERY ×2` (one per delivery station). Sudoku and Maze are separate signs now — they can live in different rooms.

## 5. Gospel & Theme

**`06-signs/gospel-box-signs.pdf`** · 6 sheets · plain — one taped to each of six boxes.
**`06-signs/gospel-completion-cards.pdf`** · **× 7 copies** = 7 sheets · **CARDSTOCK** — two straight cuts per sheet = 42 cards.

Stock each box with the cards whose sentence matches its sign: **GOD 7, PAYING 7, OUR 6, EVERYONE 6, SINS 6, LIFE 6.**
Put the six boxes in six spread-out places. **Keep the OUR box and the SINS box far apart** — both sentences contain the word "sins" and that's the one pair a 6th grader may fumble.
Counselors keep each card on verification. One adult re-stuffs the six boxes between rounds.

## 6. Sabotage (Lights Out)

**`08-sabotage/sabotage-supply-props.pdf`** · 3 sheets · **CARDSTOCK** → cut into 6 props, one cut per sheet.
**`08-sabotage/sabotage-central-command.pdf`** · 1 sheet · plain — lives on the Central Command clipboard.

Tape each prop at the door printed on it: **FUSE U1 · BATTERY U2 · KEYCARD U4 · O2 TANK D1 · WRENCH D2 · REACTOR ROD D3.**

## 7. Role cards

**`07-counselor/role-cards.pdf`** · 6 pages, **plus 4 extra copies of page 6** = 10 sheets · plain

Pages 1–5 are one per counselor — page 1 is the **GAME MASTER**, who stands by the TV and runs the clock, the dials and Undo. Page 6 (**ALL COUNSELORS**) goes to all five.

## 8. Sudoku

**`02-sudoku/sudoku-puzzles.pdf`** · 20 sheets · plain — **cut each sheet into quarters** = 80 numbered puzzles.
**`02-sudoku/sudoku-answers.pdf`** · 5 sheets · plain — 16 answers per sheet, numbered to match the slips.

Every puzzle is solver-verified to have exactly one solution. Checking by eye still works — the key is for speed.

## 9. Your own maze sheets

**80 mazes** — checked by eye, no answer key needed.

# Ten-minute pre-game check

- [ ] 7 door sheets taped up, all four corners
- [ ] 6 Sabotage props taped at their printed doors
- [ ] Tonight's target and the pew Bible edition written on all 4 answer sheets
- [ ] 6 gospel boxes stocked and spread out, OUR and SINS far apart
- [ ] Bible table set with 6 Bibles
- [ ] Cup lanes reset, apple trays down, flight zone lines taped
- [ ] Sudoku and maze stacks out
- [ ] Red balls in the bowls, one bowl at each end
- [ ] Whiteboard blank, death count at 0, tonight's threshold written beside it
- [ ] Both stopwatches zeroed — round clock (Game Master), phase timer (Central Command)
- [ ] A spoon in **every** student's hand
- [ ] Imposters briefed privately — do real tasks, get real marks, count to 20 between kills, target anyone alone
- [ ] Every counselor has their role card, the ALL COUNSELORS page, and all 4 answer sheet pages

# The two numbers you set on the night

**Target points.** Cards are worth 11. Announce the target — you don't print it.
**6 is easy · 8 is neutral · 10 is hard.** At 8 or below a student can skip their 3-point task, which is the slack that stops a jammed station locking anyone out. Above 10 there is no slack at all.

**Death threshold.** 6 for round 1 with ~17 crew — write it beside the count on the whiteboard.
Drop to 4 if the imposters got 2 or fewer kills; raise to 8 if they hit 6 before minute five.
Kills and **crewmate** ejections tick the count — an ejected imposter doesn't.
