Special Delivery

2 ptLOCKED
Lobby <-> Dead Room, both directions10
What happens
Carry one red ball between the two stations. Card names your direction. Receiving counselor takes the ball and stamps. If your pickup end is empty, fetch one from the far end and bring it back — that round trip counts.
Verify
Ball in hand at the right end. Stamp on receipt. ~3 seconds, no key.
Materials
  • 3 red balls
  • A bowl or tray at each end
Printables
  • SPECIAL DELIVERY sign — print 2, one per station (in the station-signs set)
Open
  • Benson's starvation rule makes the pool self-healing: a student who finds an empty end just re-balances it, and that IS their delivery. No adult shuttles anything
  • Throughput with 3 balls: ~12 students, 4 trips per ball, one ball leaving each end every ~2 minutes. Expect some waiting. 5-6 balls would remove it almost entirely
  • EDGE CASE: if all 3 balls are in transit, BOTH ends are empty and there's nothing to fetch. Ruling: 'No ball anywhere? Go do another task and come back.' The 16-vs-10 slack covers it
  • Killed while carrying: put the ball on the floor beside you, don't throw it. A ball next to a body tells the crew which way the victim was headed
  • Say 'no throwing, no bouncing' in the briefing — a bounced ball in a dim hallway is a trip hazard

Scoring model

LOCKED
What happens
PROPOSAL — replace 'every crewmate reaches 10' with an aggregate team target of 100 crew points, pooled, living + dead.
Materials
  • Whiteboard column for CREW POINTS beside the death count
Printables
  • Cards change from 'MY TOTAL ___ / 10' to 'MY POINTS ___' + 'Team goal: 100 — keep going past 10'
Open
  • Target is now a spoken number, not a printed one. Cards total 11, so the dial runs 6 (easy) to 11 (every task on the card)
  • 3 mediums + 2 easies = 8. So any target of 8 or below leaves the hard task skippable and preserves the anti-jam slack; 9 or above makes the hard task mandatory on all 16 cards
  • At 11 there is no slack at all — a jammed station locks a student out, which is exactly what the playbook's §7 slack was designed to prevent. Treat 10 as the practical ceiling
  • Answer sheet has a TONIGHT'S TARGET blank at the top of both pages so all four counselors are working from the same number

What happens
PROPOSAL — there is no doom timer. One stopwatch counts down from 8:00 of floor time, started when the 60-second button lock expires. Only meetings pause it. When a Sabotage resolves, if the display reads over 5:00, set it to 5:00. Ejections reset nothing.
Materials
  • 2 stopwatches
Printables
  • Central Command clock card
Open
  • One stopwatch counting down from 8:00 of floor time, started when the first 60 seconds are up
  • Only meetings pause it. Sabotage does not pause it. Body transit does not pause it
  • Sabotage succeeds +1:00 · Sabotage fails −1:30 and +2 deaths
  • There is no doom timer. If no Sabotage fires, the round ends at 8:00
  • REMAINING PLANNING NOTE: the real night is ~50 min, not 40 — 8:00 floor plus 2-3 paused 3-minute meetings is 15-18 min per round. Cap meetings at 2 per round, or publish 50-55 min

Sabotage: Lights Out

LOCKED
What happens
Supply Run. Six props live at fixed doors. An imposter taps any counselor; lights dim; Central Command reads five props AND their doors, one item per person. Two minutes.
Materials
  • 3-4 flashlights
  • Light switch access
  • Tape for the 6 props
Printables
  • 6 supply prop cards, 2 per page (FUSE U1 · BATTERY U2 · KEYCARD U4 · O2 TANK D1 · WRENCH D2 · REACTOR ROD D3)
  • 1-page Central Command script
Open
  • Naming the DOOR as well as the item turns it from a search problem into a scatter problem — 6th graders fail searches under a clock but win footraces
  • One item per person, five items from five different people. That clause is what makes the list impossible for one student to satisfy
  • Three pre-built call sets on the CC page, each guaranteed to span both floors so you never have to work it out mid-event
  • Fail = +2 deaths and −1:30, NOT an instant round loss. Against a threshold of 6, two failures put imposters at 4 without a single kill
  • Succeed = +1:00 on the round clock
  • 2 per round, none in the first 2 minutes, any counselor can be tapped
  • Each prop has its own door printed on it, so reset is handing them to 5 students — no adult chore and nothing to remember
  • OPTIONAL for round 2 only: let imposters carry off one prop as sabotage. Held back because if a hidden prop gets named, failure is automatic and the crew never learns why
